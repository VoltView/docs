Battery data follows solar. VoltView reads the inverter or battery portal. We do not charge, discharge, or change a backup reserve.

A battery belongs to a site and is mapped to the same import MPAN as the solar it sits with, if any.

## What you get

| Series | Unit |
| --- | --- |
| State of charge | % |
| Energy remaining | kWh |
| Charge and discharge power | kW |
| Charge and discharge energy in the interval | kWh |
| Status | charging, discharging, idle, or fault |

With solar and the import meter on the same site, you can see whether the battery charged from solar, from the grid, or covered a peak that would otherwise have been imported.

## Connect

Same options as solar: API token, read-only portal user, installer share, or a file export.

Hybrid inverters often expose PV and battery on one portal. One connection can populate both a solar system and a battery. They are still stored as two assets.

## Supported portals

Battery connectors ship after solar. The first targets are the same portals used for PV, including SolarEdge storage, SMA / Sungrow / Fronius hybrids, and GivEnergy or FoxESS where a site uses them.

## Not included

Remote dispatch, tariff-based optimisation on the battery, or flexibility / VPP enrolment.
